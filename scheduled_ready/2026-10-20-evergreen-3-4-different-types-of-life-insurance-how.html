### An Overview of Life Insurance EDIS Life insurance is a financial product designed to provide financial security for the beneficiaries in the event of the policyholder's untimely death. The primary purpose of purchasing life insurance is to protect the people who depend on you. Life insurance can help ensure that your family's financial needs are met even after your untimely death. It can also provide a way to leave a cash gift to your spouse, children, grandchildren, or charities.

EDIS Once you decide to buy a life insurance policy, you will need to make several decisions. You will need to select among the different types of life insurance, decide on a dollar amount, and name your beneficiaries. There are four main types of life insurance: term life insurance, whole life insurance, universal life insurance, and variable life insurance. Each type has its own unique features and benefits.

EDIS It is the most affordable option and is suitable for those who need to protect their family for a limited time. Whole life insurance, on the other hand, provides coverage for the entire duration of the policyholder's life. It is a permanent insurance policy that provides coverage for the entire term of the policy, regardless of the policyholder's health. Universal life insurance offers a combination of the benefits of term and whole life insurance.

It provides coverage for a specific period and offers the flexibility of adjusting the premium based on the policyholder's financial situation. Variable life insurance allows the policyholder to invest in the stock market, providing the potential for higher returns, but also higher risk. EDIS Determining the amount of life insurance needed is crucial. The amount of life insurance needed depends on the size of the family and the financial obligations they have.

A general rule of thumb is to have enough life insurance coverage to replace the total income of the policyholder's family. EDIS Naming beneficiaries is also an important decision. Beneficiaries are the people who will receive the proceeds of the life insurance policy in the event of the policyholder's death. The beneficiaries can be the policyholder's spouse, children, grandchildren, or charities.

It is important to name beneficiaries before purchasing life insurance to avoid any confusion or disputes in the event of the policyholder's death.
