### Liquidity Analysis of South African Companies Nelson Mandela University Research Repository In the realm of business finance, understanding the liquidity position of companies is crucial for making informed decisions. This study, conducted by Nelson Mandela University, delves into the traditional liquidity and cash flow ratios to assess the liquidity of South African companies. The research aims to identify the differences between these two types of ratios and to demonstrate that cash flow ratios provide a more accurate indicator of a company's liquidity. Nelson Mandela University Research Repository The study utilizes financial statements from four years of bankrupt companies, including the statement of financial position and statement of cash flows.

By comparing the traditional liquidity ratios with the cash flow ratios, the research aims to highlight the deteriorating liquidity positions of these companies over time. The findings suggest that traditional liquidity ratios, such as the current ratio and quick ratio, may not always provide a complete picture of a company's liquidity status. Nelson Mandela University Research Repository The research concludes that cash flow ratios, which take into account the actual cash inflows and outflows, are a better indicator of liquidity. This is because traditional ratios often do not reflect the actual cash flow situation, which is crucial for assessing a company's ability to meet its short-term obligations.

The study's findings are particularly relevant for small businesses, as they often operate with limited cash reserves and need to manage their liquidity effectively. Nelson Mandela University Research Repository The research also highlights the importance of considering the context of the company's industry and the specific circumstances that may affect its liquidity. Understanding these nuances is essential for small businesses to make informed decisions and manage their liquidity effectively.
