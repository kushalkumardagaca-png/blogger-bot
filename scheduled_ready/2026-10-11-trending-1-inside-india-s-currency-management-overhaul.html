Financial & Policy Glossary

Policy Repo Rate
The interest rate at which the central bank lends money to commercial banks, set at 5.50% as of October 2026.
Standing Deposit Facility Rate
A rate set at 5.25% used by the central bank to absorb excess liquidity from commercial banks without requiring collateral.
Marginal Standing Facility Rate
A penal rate set at 5.75% at which scheduled banks can borrow additional overnight funds from the central bank against approved government securities.
Cash Reserve Ratio
The baseline requirement of 3.00% mandating commercial banks to keep a stipulated fraction of their net demand and time liabilities with the central bank.
Statutory Liquidity Ratio
The requirement of 18.00% mandating commercial banks to maintain an equivalent proportion of assets in designated liquid instruments like government securities.
Central Bank Digital Currency
A digital form of a country's sovereign currency issued by the central bank, represented by India's digital rupee pilots.
Ways and Means Advances
Short-term temporary credit facilities provided by the central bank to governments and state sub-units to bridge cash flow mismatches.
Monetary Policy Committee
A six-member committee responsible for setting policy interest rates under a flexible inflation targeting framework.
External Commercial Borrowings
Commercial loans raised by eligible resident entities from non-resident lenders, tracked as part of forex and capital flows.
Counterparty Credit Risk
The risk that a counterparty will default on its contractual obligations before the final settlement of transaction cash flows.
Credit Valuation Adjustment
An adjustment to the price of derivative portfolios reflecting the market value of counterparty credit risk.
MCLR
Marginal Cost of Funds-based Lending Rate, representing the internal benchmark lending rate for commercial banks.
Base Rate
The minimum interest rate below which commercial banks are not permitted to lend to customers.
Call Rates
Short-term interest rates applied to overnight interbank borrowings and loans.
FCNR(B) Deposits
Foreign Currency Non-Resident (Bank) deposits maintained in foreign currencies by non-resident Indians.
Novation
The act of replacing an existing obligation or contract with a new one, such as in OTC derivative contracts.
