Precious Metals Terminology

COMEX Futures
Global derivatives exchanges where gold (GC) and silver (SI) futures contracts are traded, serving as the basis for empirical price tracking and market regime analysis.
Beta
A quantitative measure of an asset's volatility and sensitivity relative to a benchmark; for example, silver exhibits a beta of 1.41 relative to gold.
Vector Autoregression (VAR)
An econometric model used to capture the linear interdependencies among multiple time series, such as evaluating how macroeconomic shocks like OECD GDP or oil prices impact precious metal pricing.
GARCH Model
Generalized Autoregressive Conditional Heteroskedasticity models used to capture volatility clustering and conditional variance in financial asset returns over time.
Safe-Haven Asset
An investment, such as gold, expected to retain or increase in value during times of geopolitical friction, market turbulence, or economic uncertainty.
Multiple Linear Regression
A statistical technique used to model the linear relationship between a dependent variable (such as gold or silver price variance) and multiple independent macroeconomic indicators.
Consumer Price Index (CPI)
An inflation metric tracking changes in the price level of a market basket of consumer goods and services, acting as a key driver for tangible asset allocation.
Repo Rate
A benchmark central bank interest rate utilized in empirical economic models to measure the opportunity cost and monetary policy stance affecting non-yielding bullion.
Money Supply (M2)
A monetary aggregate encompassing cash, checking deposits, and easily convertible money, identified as a robust determinant of domestic gold returns in developing markets like Türkiye.
Extreme Bounds Analysis
A robust econometric estimation technique (utilizing Leamer and Sala-i-Martin frameworks) used to test the sensitivity of explanatory variables and identify robust determinants of spot gold returns.
Johansen Cointegration
A statistical procedure used to test for cointegrating relationships among non-stationary time series variables within vector error correction frameworks.
Streaming Company
A firm that acquires rights to future mineral production by providing upfront capital financing, insulating it from direct operational cost inflation while providing leverage to spot values.
Troy Ounce
The standard unit of mass used for precious metals pricing, referenced in baseline figures such as gold appreciating from $1,623 to $5,355 per troy ounce.
Nominal Return
The unadjusted percentage change in an asset's price over a specified period, such as gold's cumulative return of approximately 177% between 2022 and 2026.
Volatility
A statistical measure of the dispersion of returns for a given security or market index, tracked via annualized percentages such as gold's 20.08% and silver's 43.40%.
