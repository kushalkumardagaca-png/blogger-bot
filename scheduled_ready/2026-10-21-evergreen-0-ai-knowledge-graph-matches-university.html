### An Integrated Knowledge Graph and Digital Twin Framework for Data-Driven Industry Education in the Nylon Chemical Industry The nylon chemical industry, characterized by its long processes, complex positions, high safety risks, and strong demand for digital transformation, faces significant challenges in traditional industry-education integration models. These models struggle to dynamically respond to changes in enterprise job capabilities, lack access to real-world production scenarios, and suffer from low efficiency in multi-entity collaboration. To address these challenges, this paper proposes a data-intelligent-driven model for constructing and collaboratively governing an industry-education integration community in the nylon chemical industry (NylonChem-IEKG). This model uses a knowledge graph of the industry chain as its semantic organization core, integrating multi-source data such as enterprises, processes, positions, skills, courses, training tasks, and governance indicators.
