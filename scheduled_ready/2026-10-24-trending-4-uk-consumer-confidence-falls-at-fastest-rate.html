This downturn is attributed to a combination of factors, including rising inflation, unemployment, and a weakening job market. The real wage and productivity stagnation in the UK has been a persistent issue for over a decade, with the country's economic performance ranking poorly in international comparisons. The study by Oxford Review of Economic Policy highlights that the UK's economic performance since the global financial crisis has seen real wage growth stagnating for over fifteen years and weak productivity growth with most, but not all, of the wage stagnation overlapping with the productivity slowdown.
