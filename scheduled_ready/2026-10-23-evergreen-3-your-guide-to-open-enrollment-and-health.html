These plans are offered by private insurance companies that have contracted with Medicare. They can include additional benefits like vision, dental, and hearing coverage, as well as prescription drug coverage. To find the best Medicare Advantage plan for you, review the plan's coverage, premiums, and deductibles.

You can compare plans using the Medicare Plan Finder tool or by contacting your local Medicare office. U S News World Report Understanding the Impact of Changes When you make changes to your Medicare coverage, it's important to understand the potential impact on your premiums and out-of-pocket expenses. Similarly, dropping coverage entirely could result in a higher premium and higher out-of-pocket costs.

It's crucial to review your new coverage options and compare them with your current coverage to make an informed decision.
